# Cloud Life ICP & qualification

## Fit in one paragraph
A Dutch organisation of 300–2,000 employees (2,000–5,000 strategic; 50–300 only with exceptional fit or strong
signals) that buys IT in the Netherlands, runs a meaningful Microsoft 365 environment, carries real operational or
regulatory exposure (NIS2, DORA, sensitive data, OT), and benefits from *continuous* Microsoft security management
rather than one-off projects.

## Pass/fail checklist
- [ ] Dutch IT purchasing authority (not a branch whose IT is bought abroad)
- [ ] 50–5,000 employees, with a sourced estimate
- [ ] Not a competitor, MSP, IT consultancy, Microsoft security consultancy, sales/marketing/recruitment agency or small software startup
- [ ] Not an existing customer, active opportunity, previous rejection, opt-out or previously delivered company
- [ ] Microsoft 365 evidence at least "strongly indicated" for high-confidence qualification
- [ ] Legal person (BV, NV, stichting, vereniging, coöperatie, public body) — natural persons/partnerships need consent

## Scoring (see SCORING_MODEL.md)
Microsoft fit 30 · size 25 · industry/operational fit 15 · security & compliance 15 · Dutch purchasing authority 10 ·
buying signals 5. Fit and evidence confidence are separate; attractive industry/size alone never yields a
high-confidence qualification. Hard exclusions and contact restrictions always win.

## Service entry points
Weak Microsoft evidence → Microsoft Security Assessment. Copilot adoption → Copilot Security Assessment. AI agents →
Agentic AI Security Assessment. Purview/data governance → Purview Care (+Protect 365 when regulated data and ≥300 FTE).
Otherwise M365 present → Protect 365.

## Quality bar
Never fill a list with mediocre prospects. Report shortfalls explicitly. 60 excellent > 100 mediocre.
